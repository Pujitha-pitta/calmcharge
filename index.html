CalmCharge

Charge your EV in the cheapest hours of the night.

CalmCharge reads Sweden's SE4 day-ahead electricity prices, forecasts the hours that aren't published yet, and tells you when to charge.

Open the live app calmcharge.vercel.app

45% lower energy cost over a year of simulated overnight charging, compared with plugging in and charging straight away.

26% smaller forecast error than assuming yesterday's prices repeat, tested day by day over 365 days.

The car is a simulation. Nothing in the app controls a real vehicle or charger.
